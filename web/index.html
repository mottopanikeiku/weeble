About weeble

These are reinforcement-learning policies trained in MuJoCo Playground and running here in MuJoCo compiled to WebAssembly.

Move
Steer with WASD, the arrow keys or the touch pad, and turn with Q / E or the turn buttons. On a gamepad, the left stick moves and the right stick turns.
Interact
Dragging a robot or a prop pulls it. Push applies the slider's impulse for 0.1 simulated seconds in a random horizontal direction, and Throw (T) fires a ball from the camera.
Inspect
I shows what the policy sees and does, ¼ speed slows time, and Reset puts the robot and every prop back.

After a fall, Go1 gets up with a second policy, but G1 has no get-up policy, so its motors hold the last pose until you press Reset.

The selector says how each policy was trained, and only the rough-ground Go1 trained on the rough patch. The scenery, props, step and robot bumpers were added for the demo and the policies never saw them, although the extra bodies can still nudge MuJoCo's solver. The shove percentages come from separate flat-ground tests.
