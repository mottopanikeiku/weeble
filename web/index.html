About the simulation

I trained these policies with reinforcement learning in MuJoCo Playground. The exported networks control the robots in MuJoCo, running in your browser.

Move
WASD / arrows, or the touch pad. Q / E or the turn buttons to turn. Gamepad: left stick to move, right stick to turn.
Interact
Drag a robot body or a prop. Push applies the slider's impulse over 0.1 simulated seconds in a random horizontal direction; wait for it to finish before pushing again. Throw / T launches a ball from the camera. Its label shows launch momentum.
Inspect
I shows policy inputs, motor actions and foot contacts. ¼ speed slows time while keeping the same physics steps. Reset restores the robot and every prop.

Go1 switches to a learned get-up policy after a fall. G1 has no get-up policy: its motors hold their last pose in a full-collision model until Reset.

The selector names each policy's training conditions. The rough patch copies the training heightfield beside the flat start; only rough-trained Go1 trained on it. The scenery, props, step and robot bumpers were added for this demo. Bumpers touch play objects only.

The robot's physical parameters are unchanged, but extra bodies can affect MuJoCo's solver even before contact. Shove percentages come from separate flat-ground tests.
